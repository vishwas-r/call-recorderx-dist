Core Purpose & Security Disclosures

Learn how Call RecorderX works, why it requires specific permissions, and how your privacy is protected.

🎯 1. What is Call RecorderX?

Call RecorderX is a dedicated local utility designed to automatically record, organize, and catalog incoming and outgoing telephone calls on Android devices. Built for professionals and individuals who need reliable records of their business and personal voice conversations, the app operates strictly offline by default to guarantee absolute data privacy and security.

⚙️ 2. Why does the app require sensitive permissions?

To automate call recording seamlessly on Android's secure operating system, Call RecorderX utilizes specific system permissions. Each permission is used strictly for local automation and call log organization:

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): To capture and encode the voice call audio into lightweight WAV, MP3, AAC, or AMR files stored locally.
  • Phone State (READ_PHONE_STATE): To detect when a phone call starts and ends, allowing the app's background recording service to initiate and terminate recording sessions automatically.
  • Contacts (READ_CONTACTS): To look up phone numbers locally and map them to contact names, allowing your recorded files to be clearly labeled (e.g., "John Doe" instead of "+1234567890").
  • Accessibility Service (BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE): Required specifically on modern Android versions (Android 10+) to retrieve the incoming/outgoing caller ID to properly catalog recordings. This is a local service connector that never reads personal text inputs, passwords, or personal screen data.

☁️ 3. Google Drive Integration & Scopes

To allow secure cloud backups, Call RecorderX offers an optional Google Drive synchronization feature. By connecting your Google account, the app uses the secure drive.file scope to create a dedicated "Call RecorderX" folder on your personal Drive and upload local recordings into it. All connections occur directly and securely over HTTPS between your device and Google's servers, and we never access, view, or store any of your files.

Optional Google Drive Cloud Backups

Learn how Call RecorderX securely synchronizes your local recordings with your personal cloud storage.

Call RecorderX is fully offline and local by default. To provide optional, reliable cloud backups for your call recordings, the app integrates with Google Drive. When enabled, this feature allows you to securely save your audio files to a dedicated folder in your personal cloud.

🔐 OAuth Scope & Data Usage Disclosure:

  • Scope Accessed: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file (Create new files and folders, and open/edit files that you create with this app).
  • Purpose of Access: To create a dedicated folder named "Call RecorderX" in your personal Google Drive and upload local call recording audio files (WAV, MP3, AAC, or AMR) directly into it for backup and recovery.
  • Local Security: All user authentication credentials and tokens are saved locally in the Android device's private secure storage (SharedPreferences) and are never sent to any third-party or developer-controlled servers.
  • Direct Communication: All sync transmissions occur directly and securely over HTTPS between your Android device and Google's official Drive API servers. We do not host, monitor, access, or sell any of your call data or cloud files.

You can enable or disable Google Drive Auto-Sync, trigger a manual upload of unsynced files, or completely disconnect your Google Account at any time in the app's Settings ➔ Google Drive Backup panel.

🛠️ Step-by-Step Configuration

Follow these quick configurations to grant background services continuous operation on modern Android engines.

1

Enable Accessibility Connector

Required to detect caller ID information and trigger background recording services on Android 10+.

💡 Android 13+ "Restricted Setting" Bypass:

  1. Navigate to phone Settings ➔ Apps ➔ Call RecorderX.
  2. Tap the vertical three dots (⋮) icon in the top-right corner of the App Info.
  3. Select Allow restricted settings and verify with your PIN/Biometrics.
  4. Re-open Call RecorderX, click Enable Service, and toggle the connector switch.
2

Grant System Permissions

Allow the microphone permission (for voice capture), phone/call log permissions (to trigger recording on incoming/outgoing hook), and contacts permission (to resolve names).

3

Disable Battery Optimization

To prevent the Android background engine from terminating Call RecorderX service, disable optimization rules in: Settings ➔ Battery Optimization ➔ Call RecorderX ➔ Don't Optimize / Unrestricted.
