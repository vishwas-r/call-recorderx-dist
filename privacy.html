Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 23, 2026

1. Overview & Core Commitment

Welcome to Call RecorderX, a local call recording utility app built by Vishwas R. We respect your personal privacy above all else.

Core Policy Commitment: Call RecorderX operates entirely offline. No personal information, telephone audio files, caller identities, or device logs are collected, shared, or uploaded to any remote servers. All data processing occurs strictly on your local device.

2. Information Collection & Sharing

Because Call RecorderX is built as an offline-first tool, we enforce strict limitations on data collection:

  • Audio Recordings: All call recordings are stored locally inside the application's private storage or standard device folders under your explicit control. We do not have access to your audio files.
  • No Analytics or Telemetry: We do not include any tracking software, third-party analytics (like Firebase or Google Analytics), or advertisements in the application.
  • No Server Uploads: There are no external databases, servers, or APIs connected to this app. Your data remains entirely yours.

3. Permissions Utilized

To automate call capturing natively on the Android operating system, Call RecorderX utilizes the following system permissions. They are exclusively used for local operations:

  • Microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`): Required to capture the audio during phone conversations for creating local recordings.
  • Phone State & Call Log (`READ_PHONE_STATE`, `READ_CALL_LOG`): Used to detect active call transitions (incoming/outgoing calls starting and ending) to automatically start and stop recording processes.
  • Contacts (`READ_CONTACTS`): Used locally to look up the names associated with active phone numbers so that your recorded files can be labeled with recognizable contact names instead of raw digits.
  • Accessibility Service (`BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE`): Used strictly to identify the caller phone number of active telephone events to properly organize and group recordings. No screen content, passwords, or personal keystrokes are processed or read.

4. Children's Privacy

Our services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. Since all data remains entirely local on the device, no child data is ever transmitted or compiled by the developer.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Your call recordings remain on your device until you decide otherwise. You can delete individual or multiple recordings at any time from the app's dashboard or by clearing the application storage in system settings.

6. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any changes are effective immediately upon posting.
